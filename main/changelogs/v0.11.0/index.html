    0.11.0 — 2026-11-09

    ← Back to the changelog index

    Changed

    • Codebase and documentation conciseness overhaul. Trimmed over 1,500 lines across the codebase and documentation. Replaced verbose narrative comments with concise invariants ("why, not what") across locus-core and locus-py. Pruned superseded development memos, obsolete plans, and dated snapshot scoreboards in favor of a single source of truth across docs and configuration profiles.
    • Benchmark baselines and distortion regression snapshots refreshed. Updated baseline.json with current ICRA scenario metrics and divan_baseline.json with single-threaded kernel timings. Re-blessed Brown-Conrady and Kannala-Brandt distortion snapshots reflecting improved AprilGrid X-junction fitting accuracy (RMSE 0.28 → 0.08 px).

    Fixed

    • Split pieces get their corners back. The seeded split's opening cuts the neck where a Kalibr tag touches a connector square, which chamfers that corner; the contour's vertex then sits at one end of the chamfer, up to ~3 px along a side, and the bit grid sampled through it misses. Against Kalibr's own detections on the TUM-VI calibration frames, these tags, not blurred ones, were the decode failures: decoded from Kalibr's corners, 93-98 % of the missed tags read correctly, from the detector's quad 0.3 %. A piece's vertex now moves to the intersection of its two sides' lines when that lies outward by more than 1 px (and at most a quarter side, on sides of at least 14 px). Measured:
    • TUM-VI calib-cam1..cam4 (cam0), share of Kalibr's tags found: 97.10 → 98.62 %, 95.52 → 97.48 %, 97.51 → 98.60 %, 96.37 → 97.91 %; decode failures down 58-62 %; no wrong ids.
    • Rendered Kalibr AprilGrids (grid): 70° field angle 95.5 → 96.5 %, ink 0.4 mm 97.0 → 97.6 %, noiseless 96.9 → 97.6 %, the rest equal or up; no wrong ids.
    • Dataset suites byte-identical (only grid splits).

    Measured and not shipped: ISI equalisation of the cell samples (a per-tag PSF scale fitted on the known border, then a box-constrained deconvolution of the payload). On renders it is large (blur 0.8 px with truth corners 86 → 100 %, 12 px tags 72 → 96 %), but on the TUM-VI frames it recovers 4 % of the missed tags (about +0.1 pp), because their misses are geometric. Applying the side-line intersection to every quad (not only split pieces) moved ChArUco board coverage and percentiles for no real-data gain.

    • quad.split_fused (grid) no longer breaks rim tags into fragments. The seeded split opened fused components with a 3 × 3 erosion, which also opens borders 2 px wide. At the fisheye rim, radial compression thins a Kalibr tag's 2-cell border to about that, so the erosion broke the tag's ring into cores that were no marker; 14 % of the tags at a rendered 70° field angle were lost this way, and 5-12 % elsewhere. The erosion is now 2 × 2, the smallest that opens anything: it keeps strokes 2 px wide and still cuts the diagonal pinch where ink spread joins two squares at an X-junction (cores are labelled 4-connected). The core floor follows, 4·(2·cells/border − 2) px. Measured:
    • Rendered Kalibr AprilGrid recall: baseline 89.4 → 97.2 %, 70° field angle 77.9 → 95.3 %, 40° field angle 89.0 → 97.5 %, ink 0.4 mm 82.9 → 95.6 %, 32 px tags 94.4 → 99.7 %, 50° incidence 45 → 67 %; no wrong ids. The recovered tags' corners match the others' (median 0.017-0.022 px, p90 0.037-0.050 px).
    • Real TUM-VI calibration frames, share of Kalibr's corners also found: 86-93 % → 95-97 %; between 0.6 and 0.8 of the half-diagonal 61-65 % → 88-94 % on calib-cam1/cam2. Corners more than half a corner spacing from Kalibr's rise from 0.13 % to 0.20 % of the matched ones, mostly where locus-tag now finds rim corners Kalibr does not.
    • EuRoC cam_april (grid): recall 99.41 → 99.52 %, false positives (1) and leave-one-out error unchanged.
    • Latency, 1 thread: EuRoC +0.3 % (noise), rendered baseline +4 %, rendered 70° field angle +12 % (23 % more tags refined).
    • Dataset suites: no grid snapshot moves.

    • A refused X-junction fit whose window was off-centre is refitted. #485 refitted a fit that landed past the move limit. Any fit refused only by the move limit or the residual gate, after landing more than 0.5 px from its start, is now refitted once over a window centred where it landed, and only the refit is judged: a window a pixel off-centre holds the junction but its far side is mostly one wedge, so the model fails the residual gate. A split piece's seed sits 1 px into the neck that way. Rendered 70° field angle: recovered tags with a corner off by more than 0.5 px 5 → 2 of 122. AprilGrid board (standard): translation mean −7.5 %, p95 0.58 → 0.38 mm, rotation p99 0.076° → 0.064°; forstner within 2 %; EuRoC (standard) unchanged.

    • AprilGrid corners at the frame edge and on the smallest tags are fitted, not left at their seeds. Against rendered truth (Kalibr's AprilGrid), tags whose mean corner error exceeds 0.1 px appear only under hard geometry. In every case one corner had kept its quad seed, 0.4-3.5 px off, because the X-junction fit refused it:

    • Within a few pixels of the frame (the fisheye rim) the fit window left the image. The window is now clipped to the image. The fit stays consistent; only its robustness to a wrong edge profile needed the symmetric window.
    • On 12 px tags the window holds 21-30 pixels, under the 28-sample floor. The floor is now 3 per parameter (21).
    • At 50° incidence, seeds of foreshortened tags start 1.5-2 px off, past what one window can vouch for. A fit that lands past the move limit is now refitted once over a window centred where it landed, and kept only if that refit stays within its own limit. Tags 0.1 px inward 0.95 % → 0.47 %; every other case, EuRoC and the dataset suites unchanged.

    Measured: - Rendered 70° field angle: such tags 0.75 % inward / 0.94 % outward → 0.19 % / 0.19 %; error over the bound 3.24 → 3.09, p90 0.058 → 0.045 px. - Rendered 12 px tags: error over the bound 6.8 → 4.6, p90 0.196 → 0.098 px; tags 0.1 px inward 1.5 % → 0.8 %. - Baseline cases unchanged. - AprilGrid board (standard): rotation mean −12 %, p99 0.080° → 0.076°, translation mean −5.1 %.     Measured and dropped: a symmetric clip (a pixel kept only if its mirror is in frame) is worse at the frame edge (p90 0.059 → 0.068 px), and a move limit of 0.75-1.0 of the reach matches the refit's gain without requiring the final window to hold the junction. The remaining 70° incidence outliers are 13-16 px tags whose seeds are 3 px off on a ~1 px border.

    • The board's per-estimator inset needs four markers of its own class. BoardEstimator required four inlier markers before estimating an estimator class's photometric inset, but counted markers with no corner in that class. Since #483 moved X-junction-fit corners into their own class, a class could be held by one marker's corners and still pass. It now counts only contributing markers, as documented. AprilGrid board (standard): translation mean 0.109 → 0.104 mm, rotation p95 0.0233° → 0.0209°; high_accuracy rotation mean 0.00126° → 0.00130°; ChArUco boards within 0.13 % on their means. Measured and dropped: a marker-clustered standard error for the inset's significance test, on the grounds that a marker's corners share errors. It also drops ChArUco insets that are real: ChArUco high_accuracy translation mean +28 %, rotation p99 +52 %.

Seen from there, the X-junction test's outward probe fell short of the connector square, and the fit, though it converged within 0.025 px of the truth, had to move more than its 1 px limit. The test now reaches 0.5 px past the half-border, and the move limit is half the window's shorter reach when that is larger. The board pose puts fitted corners (corner_refined bits 4-7, new) in a third estimator class with no inset, instead of pooling them with the gradient-orthogonality corners.     • AprilGrid corners: the junction pass now reaches small tags, and the marker inset no longer misreads Kalibr's 2-bit border. Measured against rendered truth (Kalibr's printed 2-bit-border AprilGrid, grid profile) and on EuRoC cam_april:

    Added

    • Recall autopsy (examples/recall_autopsy.rs, bench-internals). For each ground-truth tag, the first stage that lost it: border not foreground, component too small, fused with another tag or other structure, no quad, decode failed, wrong id. Truth from a hub dataset or a JSONL file. On a render of Kalibr's printed AprilGrid it splits every lost tag between decoding (13–95 % per case, mostly 3–9 bits off) and ink-spread fusion (2–67 %); see docs/engineering/benchmarking/recall_autopsy_20261104.md.
    • decoder.marker_border_bits (default 1; OpenCV's markerBorderBits, Python DecoderConfig.marker_border_bits, 1–3): the printed black-border width. The detected quad is the border's outline, so a marker printed with a wider border had its bits sampled off-centre. Kalibr's AprilGrid prints tag36h11 with 2 border bits, and the 10-cell tag decoded only at the 0.9 scale retry, about 0.3 bit from its cells' edges. Declared, the payload is sampled through the outline homography composed with diag(s, s, 1), s = (d + 2)/(d + 2b) (exact under perspective). The scale retries apply to that lattice, the border evidence covers all (d + 2b)² − d² border cells, and the corner pass and marker inset size their windows and layout from the border.
    • On a physically based render of Kalibr's printed AprilGrid (TUM-VI photometry, grid, marker_border_bits = 2), against the rendered truth:
      • recall 0.536 → 0.780 at the baseline (Kalibr's extractor 0.788);
      • 0.685 → 0.912 without ink, 0.161 → 0.522 at 0.8 px blur, 0.006 → 0.300 on 12 px tags;
      • error over the Cramér–Rao bound 15.5 → 13.7 (baseline) and 13.0 → 9.2 (0.8 px blur);
      • tag-scale bias within the ±0.01 px target in every case with blur ≤ 0.8 px and ink ≤ 0.2 mm, except 70° incidence (−0.020 ± 0.027 px over ~170 corners).
    • EuRoC cam_april (grid): recall 88.0 → 88.3 %, one extra false positive (a correct id 5.2 px off the reference outline). Leave-one-out corner median 0.2423 → 0.2443 px. 1-thread latency +12 % (5.72 → 6.42 ms, serial A-B-B-A).
    • No shipped profile sets it, and with the default every dataset snapshot is byte-identical.
    • quad.split_fused (default false, on in grid; Python QuadConfig.split_fused): split components fused at their corners. Ink spread and blur close the diagonal contact where two dark squares meet corner to corner, and on Kalibr's AprilGrid every tag meets its neighbours' connector squares that way. One component then holds a tag and its connectors, or several tags, and its outline is no single marker's: it either decodes nothing or traces no quad. After the decode pass, each such component able to hold two markers is eroded by a 3 × 3 square, which opens the one-to-two-pixel necks. Every core of at least a marker's size is dilated back (a morphological opening, labelled per core), and the pieces are traced and decoded as candidates appended after the first pass, so they never displace one. The whole pass runs on runs, so its cost follows the component's run count, not its bounding box.
    • On the physically based render of Kalibr's AprilGrid (grid, marker_border_bits = 2), against the rendered truth:
      • recall 0.780 → 0.894 at the baseline (Kalibr's extractor 0.788);
      • 0.361 → 0.829 at 0.4 mm ink (Kalibr 0.736), 0.438 → 0.944 on 32 px tags (0.904);
      • 0.389 → 0.725 at 70° field angle (0.705), 0.853 → 0.991 at 0° incidence (0.992). Locus now matches or exceeds Kalibr's recall in every case. Bias stays within the ±0.01 px target (0.4 mm ink moves inside it, +0.0106 → +0.0075 px). Error over the Cramér–Rao bound is unchanged or slightly lower.
    • Real TUM-VI calibration frames (grid, marker_border_bits = 2), against Kalibr's corners on the same frames, over eight recordings and cameras:
      • share of Kalibr's corners also found: 0.29–0.52 → 0.86–0.93;
      • at 0.6–0.8 of the half-diagonal: 0.09–0.29 → 0.61–0.85. The localization-outlier rate is unchanged at about 0.14 %. The tag-scale offset against Kalibr is unchanged except on calib-cam4 (+0.002 → +0.014 px).
    • EuRoC cam_april (grid): recall 88.0 → 99.4 %, leave-one-out corner median 0.2424 → 0.2461 px, since the recovered tags are the hard ones. One false positive (0 → 1): tag 3 in the frame where marker_border_bits = 2 already had its one, 5.1 px off the reference. With marker_border_bits = 2, recall is 88.3 → 99.7 %, with false positives 1 → 2.
    • Cost: about 30 % of detect-to-decode time on EuRoC frames (in-process timing; the serial A-B-B-A wall clock gave +24–28 % under external load). On textured frames it is 33–36 % and finds nothing, which is why standard and high_accuracy leave it off. Every dataset snapshot is byte-identical.
    • Measured and not shipped:
      • running the split on every component: textured frames fill the candidate cap with pieces and push real tags out (ICRA grid recall 0.67 → 0.26);
      • geodesic regrowth over the bounding box: up to 2.6 pt more recall on the renders, but up to 424 ms on a frame-sized texture component, where the opening on runs stays bounded by the run count;
      • a 5 × 5 erosion: it opens small markers' own borders;
      • requiring two cores: a tag fused only to its connectors has one;
      • skipping a lone piece that keeps ≥ 90 % of its component: −5 % cost, but it loses up to 0.9 pt of recall.

    Changed

    • Decoding accepts a codeword past the Hamming budget when the marker's evidence supports it. Hard bits discard how sure each one is. On small or blurred markers blur flips the bits next to a bright neighbour, but those sit near their thresholds, while a non-marker contradicts a codeword with confident bits or a bright border. A match within half the family's minimum distance (5 bits for 36h11, where the nearest codeword is unique) now stands when the confidence of the disagreeing bits plus the bright border cells is at most 2 % of the marker's total; decode-first matches are verified on the refined quad by the same rule. It applies only where random bits rarely reach that radius at all (it lies within the family's recovery window: 36h11 and ArUcoMip36h12, not tag16h5), so the evidence is a second, independent guard, and never to an in-budget match whose border ring failed. The 2 % comes from rendered Kalibr AprilGrids against 102 595 candidates from tag16h5 and ChArUco renders: negatives never fell below 3.3 %, true tags' median is 0.5-1.4 %. An explicit decoder.max_hamming_error still caps it. Measured:
    • Rendered Kalibr AprilGrid recall (grid): blur 0.8 px 63.8 → 75.2 %, 12 px tags 31.9 → 45.6 %, 50° incidence 67.4 → 71.2 %, ink 0.4 mm 95.6 → 97.0 %, baseline 97.2 → 97.8 %; no wrong ids.
    • ICRA forward recall +1.0 pp (grid), +0.7 pp (standard), no extra detections; AprilGrid board tag coverage 0.9917 → 0.9924. The tag16h5, ChArUco and render-tag suites are byte-identical.
    • EuRoC cam_april: false positives unchanged (grid 1, standard 0).

    • Latency, 1 thread: EuRoC −0.5 %, rendered blur 0.8 −7 % (an accepted candidate skips the remaining scales and recovery).

    • AprilGrid X-junctions are located by a model fit (decoder.corner_subpix). A marker whose four corners are X-junctions (connector squares touching them) is re-placed by a weighted Levenberg–Marquardt fit of A + B·erf(n₁·(p − c)/s)·erf(n₂·(p − c)/s) to the raw pixels of an elliptical window spanning the black border along each edge (xjunction.rs). Gradient orthogonality read the corner from a resampled patch, whose error depends on where the corner falls within its pixel; the fit reads pixel values as they are, and its point symmetry keeps it unbiased under any point-symmetric blur and tone curve. All four corners or none, and the marker inset leaves fitted corners alone. Measured:

    • Rendered Kalibr AprilGrid (TUM-VI lens and photometry, 2-bit border, grid): median error over the Cramér–Rao bound 13.7 → 3.1 at baseline; 2.6-3.4 across ink, 32 px tags, 0.8-1.2 px blur, 50-70° incidence and 40-70° field angle; 4.4 at 0° incidence, 4.5 on 12 px tags and 7.4 at 0.3 px blur (from 7.5-18.8 before). Kalibr's own extractor reads 13-14. Recall unchanged; bias within ±0.01 px except at 70° incidence, where 32 corners give a −0.05 ± 0.02 px tag-scale bias (−0.02 ± 0.03 before).
    • EuRoC cam_april leave-one-out median 0.242 → 0.228 px (grid) and 0.243 → 0.229 px (standard), p90 0.53 → 0.49 px; recall and false positives unchanged.
    • AprilGrid board (high_accuracy): translation mean 0.026 → 0.010 mm, p95 0.104 → 0.041 mm, p99 0.29 → 0.10 mm; rotation p99 0.0186° → 0.0148°. standard: translation mean 0.166 → 0.154 mm, p95 1.02 → 0.76 mm, p99 4.23 → 4.38 mm; rotation p99 0.101° → 0.084°. Per-corner error on 29-60 px tags 0.035 → 0.010 px, on larger tags 0.034 → 0.007 px. forstner moves within 4 %. Tags under 29 px whose seeds sit ~0.8 px off stay at their seeds: replacing corners one at a time instead left such tags half fitted and raised standard's p95 translation 28 %.
    • Cost: an AVX2+FMA kernel (runtime-detected; portable fallback) keeps dense small-tag AprilGrid frames at +12 % detect time and EuRoC within noise (+1.8 %), single thread.

    • Measured and dropped: a curvature term per line for lens bow (median error +60 %), box-integrating the model over each pixel (no change), robust residual weights (worse), four free levels (no change).

    • helicoid 0.0.4. Its SO3::from_matrix returns Shepperd's quotients whenever they are within 16u of the unit sphere, so exact rotations stay unit and the chosen pose branch's quaternion matches tf_tree_math's accuracy (helicoid 0086). The seven board snapshots' float metrics move by at most 4.2e-9 relative; every detection and count is unchanged.

    • helicoid 0.0.8. Updated dependencies helicoid and helicoid-linalg to 0.0.8 (incorporating 0.0.6, 0.0.7, and 0.0.8).
    • Adopts SE3::inv_act and SO3::inv_act for inverse point action \(R^\top(p - t)\) without catastrophic cancellation; exposed as [Pose::inverse_transform_point] and [Pose::transform_point].
    • Adopts Frobenius-nearest polar rotation projection onto SO(3) via helicoid_linalg::nearest_rotation; exposed as [Pose::from_matrix_nearest] and [pose::nearest_rotation].
    • Integrates helicoid's single-rounding exact SO(3) logarithm and portable Real::atan2 kernel.
    • helicoid 0.0.5. An accuracy and speed release with no API break: the SO(3) coefficients and Log, Quat::to_matrix's diagonal, and the SE(3) Exp and Jacobians change in their last bits. The seven board snapshots' float metrics move by at most 1.9e-9 relative (67 values); every detection and count is unchanged.
